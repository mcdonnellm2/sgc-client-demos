FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How do I schedule a service or get a quote?

Use the instant estimator at the top of the page for a ballpark in about 20 seconds. For an exact number, call 843-516-4855 — we typically respond within 1 business hour.

Will high pressure damage my siding or roof?

Not with us. We use a soft-wash system: low pressure, high-volume water mixed with safe cleaning solutions that removes algae and dirt without risking damage to shingles or vinyl siding.

Why hire a professional instead of doing it myself?

Professional cleaning avoids uneven "striping" on concrete, water intrusion risks and shingle damage. Commercial-grade equipment and safer chemistry deliver deeper, longer-lasting results than a consumer pressure washer.

What areas do you serve?

All of Horry County, SC — Myrtle Beach, Conway, North Myrtle Beach, Little River, Surfside Beach, Socastee, Carolina Forest, Garden City, Murrells Inlet, Aynor and everything in between.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — Just Blast It is fully licensed and insured for both residential and commercial exterior cleaning. Insurance certificates available on request for property managers.
